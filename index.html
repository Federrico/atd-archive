rclone-atd-bus

A personal file-transfer utility.

rclone-atd-bus is a private, single-user utility used by its owner to move files between his own computers. It uses rclone, an open-source command-line program, together with the Google Drive API, to copy files from one machine to a folder in the owner's own Google Drive account and then down to another machine.

It is not a product, not a service, and is not offered to anyone else. It has no users other than its owner, no sign-up, and no interface beyond a command line.

Privacy

The application collects no data about anyone. See the privacy policy for details.
