atd-archive

atd-archive is a private, single-user utility used by its owner to archive his own files to his own Google Drive.

It uses rclone, an open-source command-line program, and the Google Drive API. Files produced on the owner's own machines are copied into archive folders in the owner's Google Drive and kept there.

  • Command-line only; there is no graphical interface.
  • Not a commercial product or a service offered to anyone else.
  • No user accounts, sign-ups or multi-user features.

The application collects no personal information. See the privacy policy.
