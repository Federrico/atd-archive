Privacy Policy

rclone-atd-bus — effective 9 September 2026

What this application is

rclone-atd-bus is a private command-line utility used by a single person — its owner — to transfer files between computers he owns. It is built on rclone, an open-source file-transfer program. It has no other users and is not distributed.

What data it accesses

When authorised, the application accesses files and folders in the Google Drive account of the person who authorises it — that is, the owner's own account. It reads and writes files in a designated transfer folder in that account. It does this solely to move the owner's files between his own machines.

What it does with that data

Files are copied from one of the owner's computers to the transfer folder, and from that folder to another of the owner's computers. Transferred files are deleted from the folder once collected. The application performs no analysis of file contents.

What it does not do

  • It does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information.
  • It does not send data to any server other than Google's own Drive API endpoints.
  • It does not share data with any third party.
  • It does not use data for advertising, profiling, analytics, or machine-learning training.
  • It has no analytics, telemetry, or tracking of any kind.

Credentials

The OAuth token issued by Google is stored in the rclone configuration file on the owner's own machines and is not transmitted anywhere else. Authorisation can be revoked at any time from the Google Account permissions page, which immediately ends the application's access.

Retention

No data is retained by the application or its developer. Files reside in the owner's own Google Drive account, under the owner's own control, and are subject to Google's retention terms rather than to any policy of this application.

Changes

Any change to this policy will be published on this page with a revised effective date.
