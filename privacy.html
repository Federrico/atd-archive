atd-archive — privacy policy

atd-archive is a private command-line utility used by a single person, its owner, to archive his own files to his own Google Drive using rclone.

Data accessed

The application is authorised with full Google Drive access to the Drive account of the person who authorises it. It is used only to list, read and write the owner's archive folders in that Drive.

How data is used

Files are copied from the owner's machines into the owner's Google Drive and kept there as an archive. The application performs no analysis of file contents.

What the application does not do

  • It does not collect personal information.
  • It does not send data anywhere other than Google Drive API endpoints.
  • It does not share data with third parties.
  • It does not use data for advertising, analytics or machine-learning training.

Credentials

OAuth tokens are stored only in the local rclone configuration on the owner's own machine. Access can be revoked at any time from the Google Account's third-party connections settings.

Retention

No data is retained by the application or its developer. Archived files remain in, and under the control of, the owner's Google Drive.
